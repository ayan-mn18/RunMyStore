# RunMyStore — speaker script

**Delivery:** warm, direct, measured; approximately 90 seconds including reading pauses.
**Audio:** Deepgram narration is pending. Use this script live; browser speech is disabled.

## Line 1 — One store. Too many jobs. (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0–8s

    You started a store. Website. Listings. Ads. Reports. The work piles up. Who connects it? You do.

**Presenter note:** Open on the merchant, not on AI. The problem is coordination: a small merchant has to carry the same product facts across separate tools and make spending decisions from separate reports. This is a problem hypothesis, not a quantified market finding.

## Line 2 — One connected workspace. (Frame 2)

**Time:** 8–16s

    Meet RunMyStore. One connected workspace. From product to storefront. From creative to campaign. With you in control.

**Presenter note:** Position RunMyStore as the connected merchant workspace. Its central value is that product context and approvals travel through the workflow. The AI actions are simulated for this hackathon UI prototype.

## Line 3 — Start with the product truth. (Frame 3)

**Time:** 16–26s

    Start with a product and the facts you know. Prepare descriptions, images and search metadata. Review the listing before it moves downstream.

**Presenter note:** Catalog Studio supports product fields, SKU validation, image input and listing preparation. The demo uses prepared output; it does not infer factual claims from a photograph. Unknown material or dimensions must be supplied by the merchant. The approved artifact is reusable downstream.

## Line 4 — A storefront that feels like you. (Frame 4)

**Time:** 26–36s

    Build a storefront around the same catalog. Edit sections, refine your brand, check mobile. Then review and publish your demo.

**Presenter note:** Use Website Builder in the live demo: edit the hero and sections, preview desktop or mobile, and review the draft. Publication creates an immutable local demo snapshot, not a public live store. Approved catalog products are available to this builder.

## Line 5 — One product. Three introductions. (Frame 5)

**Time:** 36–46s

    Turn that product into ad concepts. Compare variations for Meta, Google and TikTok. Edit the copy. Save a creative. Attach it to a campaign.

**Presenter note:** The UI creates prepared editable concepts tied to the selected catalog product, with platform previews and a versioned creative library. These visuals illustrate concept preparation, not live ads. Static artwork and carousel cards can export as PNG; generated video is currently a storyboard.

## Line 6 — See what the campaign is doing. (Frame 6)

**Time:** 46–57s

    Track spend, revenue and purchases together. See campaign status and compare channels. Seeded demo figures illustrate the decisions a merchant needs to make.

**Presenter note:** All numbers are October 1–24, 2026 fixtures from the app: $31,920 attributed revenue, $9,000 ad spend, 399 purchases. ROAS is 31,920 divided by 9,000, rounded to 3.55. This is not company traction, a causal lift claim, or a forecast. New demo campaigns begin with zero history; next-day events are simulated.

## Line 7 — Move the money. Keep the limit. (Frame 7)

**Time:** 57–68s

    Move $500 from Meta and $300 from TikTok to Google. The $12,000 monthly cap stays fixed. You review the proposal and approve the change.

**Presenter note:** This is the exact planning scenario in the app. Meta moves from $6,000 to $5,500; Google from $3,600 to $4,400; TikTok from $2,400 to $2,100. The total stays at $12,000. The recommendation responds to seeded pacing, not a proven performance optimization. Show Review, then Approve & apply, then the decision journal.

## Line 8 — Agentic by design. Human by decision. (Frame 8)

**Time:** 68–81s

    The intended orchestration layer: AgenticOrg by Pine Labs. Shared context. Specialist roles. Approval before action. Today the connected UI uses mock data. Agent execution is simulated.

**Presenter note:** Be explicit with judges: AgenticOrg is a proposed integration; current role handoffs are simulated. Proposed flow: a request carries shared product/catalog context; specialists prepare artifacts; the operator presents a diff; a merchant approves; a scoped tool executes and records a receipt. Real AgenticOrg, model and external provider calls remain next steps, not completed features.

## Line 9 — You run the business. (Frame 9)

**Time:** 81–90s

    You run the business. RunMyStore runs the store. Let’s walk through the prototype: one product, one campaign, one approved decision.

**Presenter note:** Switch to the live prototype. Start in Catalog Studio and open the draft mug. Confirm material, prepare and approve the listing. Show Website Builder, then Ad Studio, attach a saved creative to a draft campaign, and close by reviewing the budget recommendation. State that all external actions are simulated.
